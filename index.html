What GACEP is

The Global Association of Customer Engagement Professionals, founded in 1994 and known as ABPM until its 2024 rename, is the global professional body for executive briefing and customer engagement programs. Its membership runs to nearly 600 practitioners across more than 150 Fortune 500 companies, and it defines the standards of excellence the discipline is measured against. Its awards are peer-judged by that membership, which is to say by the people who run the briefing programs at the largest companies in the world.
